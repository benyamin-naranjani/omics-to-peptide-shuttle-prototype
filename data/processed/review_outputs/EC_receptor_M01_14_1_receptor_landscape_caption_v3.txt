OmiPep Module 01 / Cell 14.1 (v3) — Mouse stroke and human endothelial expression. Each point represents one mouse–human relationship from the frozen 125-row screen (114 distinct mouse genes), positioned by replicate-aware mouse endothelial D02-versus-Sham log2 fold change (x) and independent human brain endothelial median CPM (y; log10[1+CPM] transformation, axis labels in original CPM units). The complete-case plot contains 110 relationships. The 84 plotted upregulated relationships include all five highlighted discovery candidates; the selected-role symbols do not add independent records. Colors/shapes distinguish D02 FDR<0.05 upregulation and downregulation from nonsignificant changes. PROCR and TM4SF1 are highlighted as provisional design targets; SELP, ECSCR and ACKR1 are provisional alternatives, based on separate literature-informed, post-hoc biological adjudication, not a model-derived threshold. Eleven relationships lack human CPM and four lack D02 measurements; none were imputed. LRP1 is described separately as the independent benchmark because its D02 data are unavailable. Expression associations do not establish native luminal protein accessibility, receptor-mediated transcytosis, intact engineered peptide delivery, or clinical safety. Underlying per-relationship plotted and unplotted records are provided in the accompanying source CSV.
